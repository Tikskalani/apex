one-time setup

APEX needs an OpenAI (or Anthropic) API key before it can analyse a resume.

  1. Copy config.example.json (next to agent_server.py) to config.json.
  2. Put your key in OPENAI_KEY (or ANTHROPIC_KEY for Claude models). The instructions are in config.example.json.
  3. Restart python agent_server.py and reload this page.

Job agent

Apply queue

Every morning APEX searches for new jobs that fit each profile and lists the best ones here. Approve the ones you want: APEX tailors the resume to each posting and writes a cover letter, both fact-checked (about 30¢ each). Then open the posting, apply, and mark it applied. APEX never submits an application for you.

Profile

Create a profile to start: a name, the job titles you want, and your base resume.
Market radar

What the postings this profile found in the last 14 days ask for, and whether your resume or evidence vault shows it. Gaps are the most-asked skills you do not show yet: build something small, add it to the vault, and every resume after that can use it.

Evidence vault

Facts you confirmed once, used as evidence in every resume, cover letter and application APEX writes from now on: your answers to its questions, your GitHub projects, and notes you add. APEX still never states anything that is not here or in your resume.
